05 · Motion & accessibility

Motion explains change. It does not perform.

The system uses three timings and one primary easing. Reduced-motion support is mandatory.

Fast120ms

Micro feedback.

Standard220ms

Most interface transitions.

Slow400ms

Larger state changes.

Accessibility baseline

  • WCAG 2.2 AA target
  • Visible keyboard focus
  • No color-only critical states
  • No important hover-only interaction

Motion boundary

  • Primary easing: cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)
  • No ambient pulse or decorative movement by default
  • Meaning must survive reduced motion
