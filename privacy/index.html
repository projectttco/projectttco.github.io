Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026

Scope

This policy covers the Projecttt Content Pipeline OAuth application and related internal automation operated by Projecttt Co.

Google data we access

When an authorized Google account connects the application, Projecttt workflows may read or write Google Sheets and Google Drive data that the account has access to, only as required for Projecttt operational workflows.

How we use the data

  • to read and update Projecttt operational spreadsheets;
  • to locate, archive, and manage Projecttt content assets in Google Drive; and
  • to support automated content operations that remain subject to Projecttt's internal review and publishing controls.

Google user data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Storage and access

OAuth credentials and workflow configuration are kept in Projecttt's self-hosted automation environment. Access is limited to Projecttt operations and the connected Google account's granted permissions.

Retention and revocation

Projecttt retains operational data only for as long as needed for the workflows that use it. Google OAuth access can be revoked at any time from the connected Google account's security settings.
